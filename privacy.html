Privacy Policy

Ava Upload · last updated 20 November 2026

Ava Upload is a private desktop application run by Respawn Daily. It is used only to publish Respawn Daily's own videos to Respawn Daily's own accounts on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok.

What data the app uses

  • Sign-in tokens from Google (YouTube), Instagram and TikTok that you grant when connecting an account.
  • Basic account information (account name and ID) to show which account is connected.
  • The videos, titles and captions you choose to publish.

How the data is used

The data is used only to upload and publish the videos you approve and to show their status. Ava Upload does not read, collect or analyse any other content, and it does not use the data for advertising.

Storage and sharing

All tokens and data are stored locally on the owner's computer. They are not sent to any server operated by Respawn Daily and are not sold or shared with third parties. Data is only transmitted to the platform it belongs to (Google/YouTube, Instagram/Meta, TikTok) through their official APIs.

Ava Upload's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube uploads are subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Deleting your data

You can disconnect an account in the app at any time, which deletes its stored token. You can also revoke access directly: Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions, Instagram in the app settings under "Apps and websites", TikTok under "Security and permissions".
